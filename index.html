    How it works

    Lorekeeper is built to make your ideas better researched, not to make your decisions for you.

    From profile to plan

    1. You describe your brandNiche, voice, pillars and your own post data, pasted in. No account connection and no scraping, so you choose exactly what is shared.
    2. It researches liveClaude, Anthropic's AI model, runs real web searches for studies, primary texts and stories in your niche. You watch every search in the research trail.
    3. It pitches ideas that fit youEach idea explains why it suits your page, names its main accuracy risk and gives one craft tip.
    4. Every source is checkedThe server compares each cited link with the pages the search actually returned, and labels any claim it cannot match.
    5. You decideKeep an idea and it joins your content plan. Reject it and you record why. Nothing is posted or scheduled for you.

    What the source labels mean

    Found in searchThe cited page was returned by the search in this run. Still read it: a real page can be misread or oversimplified.
    Not found in searchThe AI cited a page the search never returned. Treat it as possibly made up until you check it yourself.
    No sourceNothing to check against. Verify it before you say it on camera, or leave it out.

    Where your judgement stays essential

    • Reading a source before stating its finding on camera.
    • Deciding whether an idea is really yours, and crediting who inspired it.
    • Choosing depth over engagement when the two pull apart.
